About us

Ideas become form. Form becomes light.

Creactive Studio has existed since 2015, led by owner Mahir Aliti – a trained painter with 20 years of experience in design, advertising and conceptual art. We are guided by a simple belief: good advertising doesn't need explaining – it gets seen. Every sign, 3D letter and neon we build has one purpose: to make your brand impossible to overlook.

As a studio, we conceive and deliver complete projects: from interior design for venues – restaurants, cafés, ice-cream parlours and other businesses – to printing and installing advertising. We work with a wide range of unexpected materials: large-scale wall prints, sculptures, custom objects, neon and 3D elements, to arrive at unique, creative solutions you won't find anywhere else. Design and workshop live under one roof, so every detail passes through our hands from first sketch to the lit-up result.

What we stand for

Bold in design, precise in execution, honest in price. We tell you what works – even when that means less.
