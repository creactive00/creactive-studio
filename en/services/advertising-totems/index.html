Advertising Totems

Seen from afar.

Freestanding pylons and advertising columns that guide customers to you straight from the road – day and night.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab)

A totem is your signpost and your landmark in one. We plan height, viewing sides and lighting so it reads instantly from a moving car and from a distance.

We handle structural design, foundations, electrics and installation as well. Interchangeable panels make it easy to update tenants, prices or offers later.

Ideal for

  • Petrol stations
  • Shopping centres
  • Shops & chains
  • Business parks
  • Car dealerships
  • Hotels & roadside stops

How it works

  1. 01

    Site analysis

    Sight lines, traffic, setbacks and permits – we check everything on site.

  2. 02

    Design & 3D view

    Shape, height and lighting visualised in your real surroundings.

  3. 03

    Steel & cladding

    Solid substructure, aluminium cladding, LED light boxes.

  4. 04

    Foundation & assembly

    Foundation, erection, electrical hook-up and final handover with you.

Materials & technology

  • Steel substructure

    Hot-dip galvanised and structurally calculated.

  • Aluminium cladding

    Powder-coated in your brand colour.

  • LED light boxes

    Evenly lit and energy-efficient.

  • Acrylic & polycarbonate

    Impact-resistant faces for logos and panels.

  • Digital displays

    Optional: LED price displays or screens.

  • Timers & sensors

    Light only when it's needed.
